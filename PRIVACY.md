# Cactus Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 30/08/2026*

## 1. Who We Are

**Cactus** is a desktop application for chemometrics and analytical data
analysis, developed by **Databloom srl** in collaboration with the
Department of Chemistry of the University of Turin.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data Cactus collects, why, how
it is used, and what rights you have regarding your data.

## 3. Personal Data We Collect

When you create a Cactus account, we collect:

| Data | Required |
|---|---|
| First name | Yes |
| Last name | Yes |
| Email address | Yes |
| Country | Yes |
| Affiliation (university or company) | No |
| Role (e.g. Student, Researcher, Professor) | No |

We also record **application usage time**, associated with your account,
as described in Section 6.

We do not collect the datasets you analyze inside Cactus — your scientific
data is processed locally on your computer and is never uploaded to our
servers.

## 4. How We Use Personal Data

**Account management**

Your first name, last name, and email are used to create, authenticate,
and manage your Cactus account (login, password reset, account-related
communication).

**Internal statistics**

Your country, affiliation, role, and usage time are used to produce
internal statistics about the Cactus user base and to understand how the
application is used — for example, the number of active users by country
or by role, or the average usage time. Where possible, we use aggregated
statistics (e.g. "32% of users are based in Italy") rather than
individual-level data for this purpose.

## 5. Legal Basis for Processing


- **Account management** (name, surname, email): processing is necessary
  to perform our contract with you — i.e. to provide the Cactus account
  and service you have requested (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
- **Internal statistics** (country, affiliation, role, usage time):
  processing is based on our legitimate interest in understanding and
  improving the Cactus user base and service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

We do not use your personal data for automated decision-making or
profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

## 6. Application Usage Statistics

Cactus records how long the application is used per session. This value
is associated with your account (linked to your user ID) and accumulated
over time as total usage time.

Purpose: internal statistical analysis of application usage, and
understanding how frequently and for how long Cactus is used.

## 7. Service Providers

We use **Supabase** as our technical infrastructure provider for
authentication, account management, and database hosting. Supabase acts
as a data processor on our behalf, under a Data Processing Addendum.

Your account data is hosted and processed in the **European Union**
(AWS `eu-west-3`, Paris, France).

## 8. Data Sharing

**We do not sell your personal data.**

Personal data is shared only with:

- Supabase (see Section 7), strictly to provide the service;
- authorities, where required by applicable law.

We do not share your personal data with third parties for their own
marketing purposes.

## 9. International Data Transfers

Your personal data is stored and processed within the European Union and
is not transferred outside the EU/EEA in the normal operation of the
service. Supabase's Data Processing Addendum incorporates Standard
Contractual Clauses as a safeguard, should this ever change.

## 10. Data Retention

Personal data is retained while your account remains active and for as
long as necessary to provide the service and fulfil the purposes
described in this Privacy Policy.

We may retain aggregated or anonymized statistical information that can
no longer be linked to an identifiable user for longer, for internal
statistical purposes.

## 11. Account Deletion

To delete your account and the associated personal data, contact us at
**info_cactus@databloom.it**. We will delete your account, including your
name, surname, email, country, affiliation, role, and associated usage
time, within `30` days of your request, unless retention is required
by applicable law.

## 12. Your GDPR Rights

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have the right to:

- access the personal data we hold about you;
- request rectification of inaccurate personal data;
- request erasure of your personal data;
- request restriction of processing of your personal data;
- object to processing of your personal data;
- request portability of your personal data, where applicable;
- lodge a complaint with your competent data protection supervisory
  authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at
**info_cactus@databloom.it**.

## 13. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect
your personal data, including restricting access to your own data through
database-level access controls, so that only you (and, where necessary,
Databloom) can access it.

The `service_role` administrative key used to manage the Supabase project
is never distributed with the Cactus application, its installers, or its
source code.
